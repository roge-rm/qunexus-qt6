KMI – QuNexus

QuNexus

This script provides plug and play operation of the QuNexus using firmware 2.0 and later. The QuNexus is an MPE controller and the per key tilt and pressure expression data will be available the Timbre and Press expressions in Bitwig (from the Y-Axis and Z-Axis respectively).

MPE Configuration on QuNexus

To enable MPE on the QuNexus, hold the preset/shift key and press the "MPE/CH" key twice, it should blink and indicate the number of member channels (default is 15).
